    Welcome to WikiRace,

    the race anyone can run. Get from one article to another using nothing but links.

    From: start article

    To: target article

    How to play

    • You start on the start article. Reach the target article by clicking links inside articles — no searching, no back button.
    • The little island at the top tracks your time and clicks. Tap its ⌄ button for dark mode or to quit.
    • Images open full-resolution when clicked (that doesn't count as a click). Section and footnote links are free too.
    • While you race, WikiRace explores Wikipedia's link graph in the background to find the shortest route for comparison.
